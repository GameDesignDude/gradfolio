Hello World! My name is Derek Manns, Ph.D. [Texts & Technology](https://cah.ucf.edu/textstech/){:target="_blank"} Ph.D. program with a concentration in [Digital Media].

My professional development experience and teaching interests include Game Design, Level Design, 3D modeling, and Programming.

Researcher of game design and prototyping in Unity and Unreal game engines. Experienced in level, game design, 3D modeling, and production. My experience has been seen in the classroom, Metaverse, platforms, and modding communities. Aligning course content, game design concepts, prototyping with production workflows, from concept through implementation, to keep instruction current with industry practices. Strengthening game design program and development project delivery through clear standards, creative oversight, and team leadership.

My passion for game development makes my commitment to education my priority. Don’t hesitate to ask for help on any aspect of development to which I am well versed. And if you just need some downtime, we can play some multiplayer games online.

My goal is to help future developers become a functional part of the game development community. With this goal, committment to hard work is important to the game development process. Do well.
